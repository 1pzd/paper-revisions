P24: Given a slit-lamp image x, the model predicts the cataract severity grade G ∈ {G0, G1, G2, G3, G4, G5}, where the severity is computed as the average of the nuclear color grade (NC, ranging from NC1 to NC6) and the cortical opacity grade (C, ranging from C0 to C5), both following the LOCS III standard [2], rounded up to the nearest integer. Specifically, with NC and C encoded as integers from 0 to 5 (NC1 to NC6 encoded as 0 to 5; C0 to C5 encoded as 0 to 5), the severity grade is G = ⌈(NC + C)/2⌉. Higher grades indicate more severe cataracts.
